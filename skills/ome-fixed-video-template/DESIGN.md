# DESIGN.md

## Style Prompt
Create a premium presentation-style HTML video template inspired by the OME PPT cover. The visual language should feel clean, high-end, calm, and business-oriented. Use a bright white canvas with very soft blue and violet gradients, dark navy typography, subtle orange accents for key AI words, a few metallic gold-purple gradient orbs in the corners, and a soft layered wave at the bottom. Preserve generous whitespace and avoid dense infographic clutter.

## Colors
- Primary heading: `#12243a`
- Secondary text: `#617086`
- Accent orange: `#f0642f`
- Background white: `#fbfdff`
- Soft blue-violet field: `#eef4ff`
- Line color: `rgba(18,36,58,.14)`

## Typography
- Preferred: PingFang TC / Microsoft JhengHei / Noto Sans TC
- Tone: modern, clean, high readability
- Title style: bold, centered, large, premium presentation feel

## Layout Principles
- Use large whitespace.
- Keep content centered and breathable.
- Brand sits in the upper-left corner.
- The cover title is centered with a thin underline beneath it.
- Content pages keep the same shell: brand, top title zone, decorative orbs, bottom wave.
- Use soft glassmorphism cards for content.
- Additional widget pages should still feel like presentation pages, not dense dashboards.
- Prefer 2-4 strong widgets per page; split crowded material into another scene.

## Motion Principles
- Title fades in and rises slightly.
- Cards appear in sequence.
- Decorative elements float subtly.
- Motion should feel calm and premium, not flashy.

## What NOT to Do
- Do not use saturated heavy blocks.
- Do not cram large paragraphs into cards.
- Do not switch to dark mode.
- Do not add too many icons or chart-like decorations.
- Do not break consistency with the cover page.
- Do not use widgets as a reason to shrink text until it becomes unreadable.
